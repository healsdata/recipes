# Buffalo White Bean Dip

## Ingredients

* 2 15 oz cans white beans, drained and kept separate
* ½ cup cream cheese, softened
* ⅓ cup sour cream or whole milk Greek yogurt
* 2-3 tablespoons ranch seasoning
* ⅔ cup mild or hot Buffalo sauce
* ¼ cup water
* Salt, to taste
* 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese, divided
* 3-4 green onions, chopped and divided

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Lightly grease a 9x9 baking dish.
2. Blend 1 can of beans, cream cheese, sour cream, ranch seasoning, and Buffalo sauce until smooth. Salt to taste. If too thick, add the water and blend again.
3. Pour into the baking dish. Stir in the second can of beans, half the cheddar, and half the green onions. Top with the remaining cheddar.
4. Bake uncovered 20 minutes, until bubbly.
5. Top with the remaining green onions and serve with tortilla chips, celery, and carrots.
