# Nebraskan Secret Cornbread

## Ingredients

* 3 large eggs
* 2 boxes Jiffy Cornbread mix, unsweet

One of the following:

* 1 (16 oz) bottle of ranch dressing PLUS one packet of ranch seasoning
* 1 (15 oz) jar Cheez Whiz heated in microwave to pourable stage
* 1 (15 oz) can of Manwich
* 1 (15 oz) can of red enchillada sauce


## Instructions

1. Liberally spray a 9x13-inch baking pan with cooking spray.
2. Preheat oven to 375° F.
3. Break the eggs into a large bowl and whisk until just combined. 
4. Add liquid ingredient and the two boxes of cornbread mix. Stir until combined (will be slightly lumpy)
5. Pour into the prepared pan and even out. 
6. For the ranch variant ONLY, sprinkle ranch seasoning on top.
7. Bake in the oven until golden brown, about 20-25 minutes.
