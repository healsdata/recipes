# Apple-Bottom Boilo

## Ingredients

* 1 small box raisins
* 4 oranges, quartered
* 4 lemons, quartered
* 1 tsp whole cloves
* 2 cups honey
* 2 cinnamon sticks
* 1/2 gallon apple cider
* 1 tsp whole allspice berries
* 1 2-inch piece ginger, chopped
* 2 (750 ml) bottles apple moonshine

## Instructions

1. Place all ingredients except moonshine in a slow cooker on low. Simmer 4 hours.
2. Strain out the fruit and spices.
3. Let cool to room temperature, then stir in the moonshine.
4. Transfer to mason jars; store in a cool, dry place. Serve warm or cold.
