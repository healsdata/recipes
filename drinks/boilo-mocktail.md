# Apple Cider Boilo Mocktail

### Ingredients
* 2 cups barley tea, brewed and cooled
* 2 cups apple cider
* 1/4 cup honey (adjust to taste)
* 1 orange, juiced
* 1 lemon, juiced
* 1 cinnamon stick
* 4-6 whole cloves
* 1-inch piece of fresh ginger, sliced
* Orange and lemon slices for garnish


### Instructions
1. Brew Barley Tea: Start by brewing the barley tea for 20 minutes. Let it cool to room temperature.
2. Prepare the Base: In a pot, combine the cooled barley tea, apple cider, honey, orange juice, lemon juice, cinnamon stick, cloves, and sliced ginger.
3. Heat and Simmer: Place the pot on the stove over medium heat. Bring the mixture to a simmer, stirring to dissolve the honey. Once it reaches a simmer, reduce the heat to low and let it simmer for about 15-20 minutes. This allows the flavors to meld.
4. Strain and Serve: After simmering, remove the pot from heat and strain the mixture to remove the spices and ginger slices. Discard the solids.
5. Serve Warm: Pour the boilo mocktail into mugs or heat-resistant glasses. Garnish with orange and lemon slices.
6. Optional: Adjust the sweetness by adding more honey if needed. Feel free to experiment with the proportions to suit your taste.
