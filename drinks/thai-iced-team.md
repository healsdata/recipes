# Lazy Thai-Inspired Iced Tea Cold Brew

## Ingredients

* 4 cups water
* 5 black tea bags (Assam or Ceylon)
* 2 pandan tea bags
* 8 tbsp. Lakanto Simple Flavoring Syrup (Vanilla)
* 8 tbsp.

## Instructions

1. Combine water and tea bags in container. Refrigerate and leave 12-16 hours.
2. Remove tea bags, add syrup and milk.

## Notes

* A bit of citric acid and cocoa powder was recommended; skipping in v1.
