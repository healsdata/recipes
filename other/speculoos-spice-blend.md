# Speculoos Spice Blend

## Ingredients

* 8 teaspoons ground cinnamon
* 3 teaspoons ground nutmeg
* 3 teaspoons ground cloves
* 2 teaspoons ground ginger
* 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
* ½ teaspoon ground anise (optional)
* 1 teaspoon ground white pepper (sub black pepper, a bit spicier)

## Instructions

1. Whisk all spices together in a small bowl.
2. Store in an airtight jar in a cool, dry place.
