# Peanut Caramel Overnight Oats

## Ingredients

* 50g oat flour (or oats blended to flour, or regular oats)
* 30g vegan vanilla protein powder (important for the thick texture)
* 100g Greek yogurt or skyr
* 5g milled flax seeds
* 10g peanut butter powder
* 90ml water or milk

### Caramel Layer

* 5-10g peanut butter
* 5-10g maple syrup

### Shell

* 20g dark chocolate
* Peanuts (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mix the oat ingredients and place in a ramekin or bowl. Refrigerate 1-2 hours to thicken.
2. Mix peanut butter and maple syrup, then spread over the oats.
3. Melt chocolate in the microwave and drizzle over the top to form a shell. Add peanuts if using.
4. Refrigerate overnight.

## Notes

* No protein powder? Use 70g oats and add maple syrup to taste.
