# Peanut Sauce Butter Beans

## Ingredients

* 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
* 3/4 to 1 cup water
* 3 tbsp reduced sodium soy sauce
* 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
* 1/2 tbsp sriracha
* 1/2 inch ginger, grated
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 15oz jar butter beans, drained and rinsed
* 1/2 lime, juiced
* Chili crisp, cilantro, sesame seeds, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Whisk peanut butter, water (start with 3/4 cup, add more to thin), soy sauce, sesame oil, sriracha, ginger, and garlic in a skillet over medium heat. Simmer, whisking, 2-3 minutes until thick and creamy.
2. Add butter beans, stir to coat. Cook 2-3 minutes to warm through. Off heat, stir in lime juice.
3. Garnish and serve with naan or pita, or over rice with veggies.
