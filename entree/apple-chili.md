# Apple Chili 

## Ingredients

* 1 large red onion, diced
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 large red apple, diced 
* 15 oz kidney beans, drained and rinsed
* 15 oz pinto beans, drained and rinsed 
* 15 oz fire roasted tomatoes 
* 2 cups vegetable stock
* 1 cup apple cider 
* 1/2lb ground pork
* 1 6oz can tomato paste
* 1 can mild green chilis
* ½ tablespoon chili powder
* ½ tablespoon smoked paprika (sub for regular, we just like the smoky flavor)
* ½ tablespoon cumin
* ½ tablespoon cinnamon
* ½ teaspoon salt
* ½ teaspoon pepper

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients into a slow cooker and stir well. Place lid on slow cooker. 
2. Cook on high for 3 hours or low for 6 hours.
