# Gluten-Free Shepherd's Pie

## Ingredients

* 2 slices bacon, preferably nitrate free
* 1 pound ground beef
* 1/2 cup coarsely chopped onion
* 1/2 bell pepper, coarsely chopped
* 1 medium carrot, coarsely chopped
* 1 cup frozen peas
* 2 tablespoons corn starch
* 1 tablespoon tomato paste
* 1 tablespoon gluten-free Worcestershire
* 1/2 to 3/4 cup beef stock
* 2 teaspoons fresh thyme leaves
* 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh parsley
* 24 ounces mashed potatoes or mashed cauliflower
* 3 ounces shredded cheddar cheese
* 1 tablespoon thinly sliced fresh chives
* Salt, to taste
* Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Fry bacon until crisp. Remove and chop. Set aside.
2. Add ground beef, onion, bell pepper, and carrot to the pan with bacon fat. Fry over medium-high heat until browned. Drain.
3. Add frozen peas, cook about 3 more minutes.
4. Stir in corn starch to thicken.
5. Stir in tomato paste, Worcestershire, beef stock, thyme, and parsley. Cook until thick and vegetables are tender. Remove from heat.
6. Preheat oven to 375°F.
7. Mix mashed potatoes, cheddar, bacon, and chives. Salt and pepper to taste.
8. Spoon meat and vegetable mixture into a 1 1/2-quart baking dish.
9. Spread mashed potatoes over the top; smooth with a spatula (fork pattern optional).
10. Bake about 30 minutes, until potatoes are golden brown.
