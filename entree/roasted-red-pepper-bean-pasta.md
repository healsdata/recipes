# Roasted Red Pepper and Bean Pasta

## Ingredients

* 250g pasta, uncooked
* 2 red peppers, roughly chopped
* 300g tomatoes
* 5 garlic cloves
* 1 generous teaspoon dried basil
* 100g ricotta
* 1 tablespoon olive oil
* 1 400g tin cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
* 20g parmesan, grated (plus extra to serve)
* Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Add peppers, tomatoes, garlic, and basil to a roasting dish. Season with salt and pepper, drizzle with olive oil, and toss.
3. Roast about 25 minutes, until softened and starting to char.
4. Let cool, then blend with the ricotta until smooth.
5. Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain, reserving a mug of pasta water.
6. Heat the sauce in a pan. Add pasta, beans, parmesan, and a splash of pasta water. Stir and cook a few minutes until the parmesan melts and everything is heated through.
7. Season to taste and serve with extra parmesan on top.
