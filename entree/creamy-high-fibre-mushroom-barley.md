# Creamy High-Fibre Mushroom Barley

## Ingredients

* 1.5 cups (250g) quick-cook barley
* 1 onion, diced
* 250g mushrooms, sliced
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 can white beans (cannellini or navy), drained
* 2.5 cups vegetable stock (or mushroom stock)
* 1/4 cup nutritional yeast
* Fresh thyme sprigs
* Salt and olive oil to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat a splash of olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Toast the barley for 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
2. Add the onion, mushrooms, garlic, and a pinch of salt. Sauté until softened.
3. Stir in the beans, stock, nutritional yeast, and thyme sprigs. Cover, reduce to medium, and simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Once the liquid is absorbed, remove the thyme sprigs, salt to taste, and top with fresh thyme leaves.
