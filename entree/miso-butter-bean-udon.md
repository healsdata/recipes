# Miso Butter Bean Udon

## Ingredients

* 1 jar butter beans, with liquid
* 150g udon noodles (or noodles/pasta of choice)
* 1 heaped tablespoon white miso paste
* 2 teaspoons sesame oil
* 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
* Juice of ½ lemon
* 2 tablespoons nutritional yeast (optional)

### To Serve

* 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
* Sesame seeds
* Chili oil
* Crispy onions

## Instructions

1. Cook noodles according to package directions.
2. Meanwhile, blend the beans, miso, sesame oil, garlic, lemon juice, and nutritional yeast with a splash of water until smooth, about the consistency of heavy cream.
3. Pour into a pan over medium heat and simmer gently 2-3 minutes until warmed through (optionally swirl in a teaspoon of chili oil).
4. Drain noodles, add to the pan, and toss to coat. Adjust seasoning, adding a squeeze of lemon if you like.
5. Divide between two bowls and top with spring onions, sesame seeds, chili oil, and crispy onions.
