# Carl's Kickin Chili Copycat

## Ingredients

### The Meat & Base

* 2 pounds coarsely ground chuck or hand-diced beef (cut into 1/2-inch cubes if hand-dicing)
* 3 ribs celery, diced fine
* 2 large raw sweet onions, diced fine (divided)
* 4 cloves garlic, minced

### The Liquid & Flavor Builders

* 1 bottle (12 oz) dark beer, stout or porter
* 3 tablespoons brown sugar or molasses
* 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
* 2 cups beef broth
* 1 small can (6 oz) tomato paste
* 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes

### The Spices & Thickener

* 3 tablespoons chili powder
* 1 tablespoon cumin
* 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
* 3 tablespoons masa harina (mixed with 3 tablespoons of warm water to make a slurry)
* Salt and pepper, to taste
* Hot peppers, to taste (fresh jalapeños, habaneros, or cayenne pepper, your choice)


## Instructions

1. Heat a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook until it forms a nice brown crust. Drain off any excess grease, leaving just a little bit in the pot for flavor.
2. Lower the heat to medium. Stir in the garlic, half of your diced sweet onions, and half of your diced celery. Cook for 5 minutes until soft.
3. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and unsweetened cocoa powder. Cook for 1 minute. The chocolate and spices will toast and smell amazing.
4. Pour in the dark beer, using a wooden spoon to scrape up all the delicious browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Let it simmer for 3 minutes.
5. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, beef broth, and brown sugar or molasses. Add your hot peppers here. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 45 to 60 minutes until the beef is very tender.
6. Pour in the masa harina slurry. Stir well and let it simmer uncovered for 10 minutes. The chili will turn beautifully glossy and thick
7. 5 minutes before serving, turn off the heat and stir in the remaining raw sweet onions and celery.
