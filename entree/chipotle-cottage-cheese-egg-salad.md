# Chipotle Cottage Cheese Egg Salad

## Ingredients

* 6 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
* 100g cottage cheese
* 30g light mayo
* 1 roasted red pepper, chopped
* 1 teaspoon chipotle paste
* Juice of ½ lemon
* 1 teaspoon paprika
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 1 teaspoon black pepper
* 1 teaspoon maple syrup
* 2 tablespoons chopped chives or parsley
* Chili flakes (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mix cottage cheese, mayo, chipotle paste, lemon juice, paprika, salt, pepper, and maple syrup in a bowl.
2. Add eggs, roasted pepper, and herbs. Mix well.
3. Top with chili flakes for extra heat.

## Notes

* Approx. per serving: 306 calories, 26g protein, 10g carbs, 18g fat
