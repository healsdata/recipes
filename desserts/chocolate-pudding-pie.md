# Classic Chocolate Pudding Pie

## Ingredients

* 1 3.9 oz box Jell-O Instant Chocolate Pudding & Pie Filling
* 1-3/4 cups cold milk
* 1 Honey Maid graham pie crust
* Cool Whip whipped topping, for serving

## Instructions

1. Whisk pudding mix and cold milk together for 2 minutes.
2. Pour immediately into the graham pie crust.
3. Refrigerate at least 1 hour, or until set.
4. Top each serving with 2 tbsp Cool Whip.

## Notes

* For a fuller pie, use 2 boxes pudding mix and 2-3/4 cups milk; chill at least 3 hours.
* Derived from the recipe printed on the Jell-O Instant Chocolate Pudding & Pie Filling box.
* Dad's favorite dessert.
