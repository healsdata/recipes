# Low Cal Carrot Cake

## Ingredients

Cake:

* 300g low-fat cottage cheese
* 2 large eggs
* 150g egg whites
* 200g carrot, finely grated
* 100g oat flour (just blend whole oats)
* 2 tsp baking powder
* 2 tsp vanilla extract
* 2.5–3 tsp cinnamon
* ½ tsp ground ginger
* ¼ tsp nutmeg
* ¼ tsp salt
* sweetener to taste, liquid or powder (be generous, it needs it)

Cream cheese frosting:

* 150g light cream cheese
* 200g 0% Greek yogurt
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* sweetener to taste, liquid or powder
* pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a 9x9 baking dish.
2. Blend everything for the cake except the carrot until smooth.
3. Fold in the carrot by hand.
4. Spread into the dish and bake 28–35 minutes, until the center is set with moist crumbs (start checking at 25).
5. Cool completely.
6. Mix the frosting ingredients until smooth, spread over the cake, and cut into 9 squares.

## Notes

* About 160 calories, 13g protein, 15g carbs, 5g fat per square
* Keeps in the fridge up to 4 days
* Derived from [Low Cal Carrot Cake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IkCpOQtWlpE)
