# Apple Cider Donut Rice Krispies

## Ingredients

* 1 cup apple cider
* ⅓ cup unsalted butter
* ½ tsp cinnamon
* ¼ tsp salt
* 12 oz mini marshmallows
* 5 cups Rice Krispies
* 2 Tbsp granulated sugar
* ¾ tsp cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Grease an 8x8 pan, line with parchment, and set aside. Put the Rice Krispies in a large bowl.
2. Simmer the apple cider in a small saucepan, stirring occasionally, until reduced to 2–3 tablespoons and thick enough to coat a spoon, about 18–20 minutes.
3. In a separate saucepan, brown the butter over low-medium heat until the milk solids turn amber.
4. Immediately stir in the reduced cider, ½ tsp cinnamon, and salt.
5. Add the marshmallows and stir over low-medium heat until fully melted.
6. Pour over the Rice Krispies and gently fold until evenly coated.
7. Press into the prepared pan and let cool slightly.
8. Mix the sugar and ¾ tsp cinnamon and sprinkle over the top. Slice and serve.
