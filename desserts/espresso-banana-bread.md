# Espresso Banana Bread

## Ingredients

* ½ cup unsalted butter, room temperature
* 1 cup granulated sugar
* 3 teaspoons instant espresso powder
* 2 tablespoons water
* 2 medium bananas, mashed (about 1 cup)
* 2 cups all-purpose flour (1:1 gluten-free flour works)
* 1 teaspoon kosher salt
* 2 large eggs
* 1 teaspoon baking soda
* ½ cup sour cream or unflavored Greek yogurt
* ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Espresso Glaze

* 2 tablespoons butter, melted
* 2 cups powdered sugar
* 2 teaspoons instant espresso powder
* 2-3 tablespoons milk

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9x5 loaf pan with parchment or grease it.
2. Beat butter and sugar 3 minutes. Add eggs one at a time, mixing after each.
3. Mix in bananas and sour cream.
4. Dissolve espresso powder in the water, then add with the vanilla. Mix well.
5. Whisk flour, salt, baking soda, and cinnamon in a separate bowl. Fold into the wet ingredients until just combined.
6. Pour into the pan and bake 45-50 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Cool completely, about 1 hour.
8. For the glaze, combine melted butter, powdered sugar, espresso powder, and 2 tablespoons milk until smooth (add more milk as needed).
9. Drizzle over the cooled loaf and slice.
